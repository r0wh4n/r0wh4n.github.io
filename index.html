AI agents do much of the typing. I own what ships. For an early team that means a working prototype in days, with infrastructure, monitoring and security handled by the same person.

agents

Several agents, one reviewer

Claude Code, Codex and Cursor run separate tasks in parallel. I review the diffs before they merge, and changes that touch production ship with tests and a rollback path.

tooling

guardrails

Secret scanning, least-privilege CI and branch protection come standard. At CARS24 I also added guardrails to the internal LLM gateway.
