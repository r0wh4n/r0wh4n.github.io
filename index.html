Hey Buddy

A voice assistant that runs your Mac.

Say the wake word and it transcribes on-device with Whisper, plans with an LLM, and acts through AppleScript and in-browser JavaScript. It starts while you’re still talking, and asks for a spoken yes before anything risky.
